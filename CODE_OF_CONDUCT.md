# Code of Conduct

Contributors must communicate professionally, discuss ideas on their merits and make room for differing experience and perspectives. Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate humiliation, doxxing and publication of another person’s confidential information are not acceptable.

Security reports, customer data, credentials and private operational evidence must not be posted publicly. Follow `SECURITY.md` for vulnerability reporting.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove material that violates these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. A concern may be raised privately through the security contact. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately and avoid unnecessary disclosure about affected people.
